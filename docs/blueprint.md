# **App Name**: PetPal

## Core Features:

- Pet Profiles: Manage pet profiles, including name, breed, age, and photo.
- Medication Tracker: Record and track medication details, dosages, and schedules.
- Appointment Log: Maintain a log of past and future appointments with vet details.
- AI Symptom Checker: AI powered tool that assists the user in identifying the potential cause of their pet's health issues based on user entered symptoms.
- Retail Store: Enable purchasing of retail items from a catalog with user accounts

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft, calming blue (#64B5F6) to promote trust and reliability.
- Background color: Light, desaturated blue (#E3F2FD) to maintain a clean and airy feel.
- Accent color: Muted orange (#FF8A65) to highlight key actions and important information.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif that is both modern and welcoming
- Use clean, minimalist icons that are intuitive and easy to understand.
- Implement a responsive layout that adapts seamlessly to different screen sizes.
- Use subtle transitions and animations to provide feedback and enhance user experience.
